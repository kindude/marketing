About SDND Marketing

A performance-only growth agency, built on a simple belief.

SDND Marketing is a performance-only growth agency, built on a simple belief: marketing should pay for itself.

We've spent the last three years working with DTC brands, subscription businesses, and e-commerce companies across Europe and North America — running paid advertising across Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, Amazon, and Reddit.

Our model is straightforward. We work on a percentage of revenue — no retainers, no fixed monthly fees. We grow when you grow.

We're not a full-service agency. We don't do PR, SEO, or social media management. We do one thing: paid acquisition, done properly.

We only take on clients we genuinely believe we can help. Our revenue depends on yours.
FAQ

Frequently asked.

Everything you'd ask in the first ten minutes of a call.

How does your pricing model work?
We work on a percentage of revenue — no retainer, no fixed monthly fee. Our fee is agreed upfront as a percentage of the revenue we generate for you. If we don't deliver, we don't earn.
Which channels do you run?
Meta (Facebook & Instagram), Google Ads, TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Amazon Ads, and Reddit Ads. We recommend the right channel mix based on your audience, margins, and goals.
What kind of brands do you work with?
DTC e-commerce brands, health and wellness companies, subscription businesses, and local brands looking to grow internationally. We work across Europe, the United States, and Canada.
Do you require a minimum ad spend?
We don't have a universal minimum — it depends on the channel and the brand. We'll tell you honestly in our first call whether your current budget is enough to see meaningful results.
What does the onboarding process look like?
We start with an audit of your current setup — tracking, ad accounts, landing pages, and performance data. Then we build a bespoke strategy and launch within an agreed timeline. Most clients are live within two to three weeks.
Do you offer creative services?
We don't produce creative in-house, but we provide detailed creative briefs and strategic direction. We also have trusted creative partners we can refer you to.
How do you measure and report results?
Revenue generated, CAC, ROAS, MER, and customer lifetime value where applicable. Clear, honest reporting — no vanity metrics.
What markets do you operate in?
Primarily Europe (UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, and more) and North America (USA and Canada). We've launched campaigns across 10+ international markets.
